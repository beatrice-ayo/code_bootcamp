Why we're building this

Becoming a software developer can help others in ways you could never quite imagine — and it can inspire others to do the same. Naija to UK exists for one reason: Nigerians arriving in the UK for the first time deserve better than piecing their new life together from a dozen different tabs.

Moving to a new country can be exciting, but it can also be confusing and genuinely stressful, especially when the language, the weather and the whole way of life feel completely different. We want this site to hand you the knowledge and context to feel steadier from day one, not weeks in.

So instead of searching endlessly across different sites and forums, our aim is simple: one place with everything a new arrival needs, and then some — built to make people feel a little more confident and a little more prepared, even if it's just a little bit.

A simple, easy-to-use website for Nigerians, so they're ready to start their new life in the UK.
